# Original sampler demonstration library

These seven single-note and single-hit WAV files are generated entirely by
`scripts/generate-sampler-fixtures.py` in this repository. No source recording,
manufacturer sound library, musical phrase, or third-party sample is used.

The generated audio is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

22,050 Hz, 16-bit mono. Pitched roots: reed/pluck C4 (MIDI 60), bass C2
(MIDI 36). These are functional demo sounds, not a selected 19-2000 palette.

The air-key-c5 and finger-bass-c2 single notes are also original CC0 assets, reproducible with scripts/generate-song-notes.py. They contain no recording, riff or stem from Gorillaz or Teenage Engineering. They are available to every sampler track through the studio folder.

The four `settler` folder tones are original oscillator recordings generated by
`scripts/generate-project-lee-samples.py` and dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0. Their roots are G#4, G#4, G#2 and G#5. They contain no audio from
DJ Settler's recording, no phrase, and no backing loop.

`settler/portamento-lead-gs5.wav` is an additional original CC0 oscillator
single note, generated by `scripts/generate-project-lee-lead.py`. It contains
no pitch sequence, transition or reference audio; the device applies all
notes and portamento during playback. The existing four assets are unchanged.

### Project Lee hard kick

`settler/hard-kick.wav` is an original synthesized oscillator/noise hit, generated
by `scripts/generate-project-lee-kick.py`. Dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0. No recorded song audio is included.

### Still D.R.E. piano, bass and drums

`dre-piano/*.mp3` (GM program 2 "Electric Grand", E4 to C6 a major third
apart), `dre-bass/*.mp3` (GM program 87 "Bass & Lead", C1 to C2) and
`dre-kit/kick.wav` and `dre-kit/snare.wav` (the "Power" drum kit, keys 35 and
40) are single notes rendered from the GeneralUser GS SoundFont v1.471 by S.
Christian Collins with spessasynth_core, one note per file with no effects
(`scripts/render-song-samples.mjs still-dre`). GeneralUser GS may be used
without restriction for music creation, private or commercial.

`dre-kit/closed-hat.wav` is the Sonic Pi sample `hat_gump` from the
`supersonic-scsynth-samples` npm package (freesound.org sound 96144 by zgump,
placed in the public domain under CC0 1.0; see that package's PROVENANCE.md),
converted to 44.1 kHz mono WAV.

The earlier generated `dre/*.wav` sources (`scripts/generate-still-dre-samples.py`,
CC0) are no longer used by the song and are not listed in the sample library.
No recorded song audio is included in any of these files.

### Numb / Encore synth, bass and drums

`numb-synth/*.mp3` (GM program 90 "Polysynth") and `numb-bass/*.mp3` (GM
program 38 "Synth Bass 1") are single notes rendered from the GeneralUser GS
SoundFont v1.471 by S. Christian Collins with spessasynth_core, one note per

may be used without restriction for music creation, private or commercial.

`numb-kit/kick.wav`, `numb-kit/snare.wav` and `numb-kit/closed-hat.wav` are the
Sonic Pi samples `bd_klub`, `sn_generic` and `drum_cymbal_closed` from the
`supersonic-scsynth-samples` npm package (freesound.org sources placed in the
public domain under CC0 1.0; see that package's PROVENANCE.md), converted to
44.1 kHz mono WAV.

The earlier generated `numb/*.wav` sources (`scripts/generate-numb-encore-samples.py`,
CC0) are no longer used by the song and are not listed in the sample library.

### Recorded orchestral instruments (strings, choir, contrabass, cello, orchestra kit)

`strings/*.mp3`, `choir/*.mp3` and `orchestra-kit/*.mp3` are single notes
rendered from the GeneralUser GS SoundFont v1.471 by S. Christian Collins
(programs "St. Strings Fast", "Concert Choir" and the orchestra drum kit),
one note per file with no effects, using spessasynth_core. GeneralUser GS may
be used without restriction for music creation, private or commercial, and
may be modified and redistributed (its license text ships with the
`generaluser` npm package; http://www.schristiancollins.com).

`contrabass/*.mp3` and `cello/*.mp3` are single recorded notes from
tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus Brosowsky (npm packages
`tonejs-instrument-contrabass-mp3` and `tonejs-instrument-cello-mp3`,
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), trimmed and faded. The
packages are published under MIT; the project credits the original sample
sources under CC BY 3.0, which is honoured here by this attribution.
