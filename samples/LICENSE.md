# Sample library provenance

The seven single-note and single-hit WAV files under `demo/` are generated
entirely by `scripts/generate-sampler-fixtures.py` in this repository. Those
seven files use no source recording, manufacturer sound library, musical
phrase, or third-party sample.

The generated audio is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

22,050 Hz, 16-bit mono. Pitched roots: reed/pluck C4 (MIDI 60), bass C2
(MIDI 36). These are functional demo sounds, not a selected 19-2000 palette.

The air-key-c5 and finger-bass-c2 single notes are also original CC0 assets, reproducible with scripts/generate-song-notes.py. They contain no recording, riff or stem from Gorillaz or Teenage Engineering. They are available to every sampler track through the studio folder.

The four `songs/mario/voices/*.wav` files are original CC0 oscillator and
noise sounds generated by `scripts/generate-mario-voices.py`. They contain no
Nintendo recording or sequence. The song's notes are entered by the device
build and remain editable in the project.

`songs/project-lee/oscillators/portamento-lead-gs5.wav` is an original CC0 oscillator single note,
generated by `scripts/generate-project-lee-lead.py`. It contains no pitch
sequence, transition or reference audio; the device applies all notes and
portamento during playback.

`songs/project-lee/oscillators/rolling-bass-gs1.wav` is an original CC0 oscillator single note (root
G#1), generated by `scripts/generate-project-lee-rhythm.py`. It contains no
audio from DJ Settler's recording, no phrase, and no backing loop.

### Project Lee kick

`songs/project-lee/kit/kick.wav` is a single hit (key 36 of the "Dance" drum kit) rendered
from the GeneralUser GS SoundFont v1.471 by S. Christian Collins with
spessasynth_core, no effects (`scripts/render-song-samples.mjs lee`).
GeneralUser GS may be used without restriction for music creation, private or
commercial. No recorded song audio is included.

`songs/still-dre/piano/*.mp3` (GM program 2 "Electric Grand", E4 to C6 a major third
apart), `songs/still-dre/bass/*.mp3` (GM program 87 "Bass & Lead", C1 to C2) and
`songs/still-dre/kit/kick.wav` and `shared/power-snare.wav` (the "Power" drum kit, keys 35 and

Christian Collins with spessasynth_core, one note per file with no effects
(`scripts/render-song-samples.mjs still-dre`). GeneralUser GS may be used
without restriction for music creation, private or commercial.

`songs/still-dre/kit/closed-hat.wav` is the Sonic Pi sample `hat_gump` from the
`supersonic-scsynth-samples` npm package (freesound.org sound 96144 by zgump,
placed in the public domain under CC0 1.0; see that package's PROVENANCE.md),
converted to 44.1 kHz mono WAV.

No recorded song audio is included in any of these files.

### Recorded orchestral instruments (strings, choir, contrabass, cello, orchestra kit)

`instruments/strings/*.mp3`, `instruments/choir/*.mp3` and `instruments/orchestra-kit/*.mp3` are single notes
rendered from the GeneralUser GS SoundFont v1.471 by S. Christian Collins
(programs "St. Strings Fast", "Concert Choir" and the orchestra drum kit),
one note per file with no effects, using spessasynth_core. GeneralUser GS may
be used without restriction for music creation, private or commercial, and
may be modified and redistributed (its license text ships with the
`generaluser` npm package; http://www.schristiancollins.com).

`instruments/contrabass/*.mp3` and `instruments/cello/*.mp3` are single recorded notes from
tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus Brosowsky (npm packages
`tonejs-instrument-contrabass-mp3` and `tonejs-instrument-cello-mp3`,
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), trimmed and faded. The
packages are published under MIT; the project credits the original sample
sources under CC BY 3.0, which is honoured here by this attribution.
