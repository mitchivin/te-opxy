# Recorded sound candidates

These are isolated instrument notes, not recordings from the songs.

## Piano
- Source: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples, Lawrence Fritts / Electronic Music Studios.
- Steinway Model B, Evan Mazunik, recorded November 2001 by Michael Cash.
- https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISpiano.html
- Originals: Piano.mf.E4.aiff and Piano.ff.E4.aiff.
- Permission: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html states the recordings may be downloaded and used for any projects without restrictions, explicitly including application developers. Checked 2026-09-22.
- Changes: trimmed leading silence with 2 ms pre-roll, cropped to four seconds, peak normalized to 0.75, final 40 ms fade, converted to stereo 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV. No musical phrase, EQ or pitch change.

## Melodica
- “melodica-c4.wav” by fese, https://freesound.org/people/fese/sounds/23034/
- Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source used: public high-quality MP3 preview, https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/23/23034_113153-hq.mp3 . This is a lossy derivative, not the original 24-bit WAV.
- Changes: decoded to mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV, peak normalized to 0.65, final 20 ms fade.
- Pitch analysis: approximately 525 Hz; MIDI root 72 (C5), despite source filename C4. No claim that it is the instrument used on the record.
